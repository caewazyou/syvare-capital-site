Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Syvaré Capital Family & Partners Limited ("Syvaré Capital", "we", "us" or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information that may be collected when you visit or interact with our website.

1. Information We May Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us, including your name, email address, contact details and any information contained in communications you send to us.

We may also receive limited technical information when you use our website, such as browser type, device information, IP address and general website usage information where this is provided through our hosting, security or analytics services.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information we receive to respond to enquiries, communicate with you, operate and improve our website, maintain website security, understand how our website is being used and comply with applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

3. LinkedIn and Social Media Content

Our website may display or link to content published by Syvaré Capital on LinkedIn or other social media platforms. Where such integrations are used, information may be retrieved through services or application programming interfaces provided by the relevant platform.

Syvaré Capital intends to use such integrations primarily to display its own publicly available company content on this website. When you choose to interact with LinkedIn content, you may be directed to LinkedIn and your activity will be subject to LinkedIn's own terms and privacy practices.

4. Market Data and Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services to provide market information, charts, financial data, maps, social media content or other functionality.

These third-party services may process certain technical information in accordance with their own privacy policies. Market information displayed on this website is provided for general informational purposes and should not be considered investment, financial or other professional advice.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary for website functionality, security, analytics or third-party integrations.

Where required by applicable law, appropriate information and choices regarding non-essential cookies will be provided.

6. Sharing of Personal Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with trusted service providers where reasonably necessary to operate our website, provide our services, maintain security or comply with applicable legal obligations.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including satisfying applicable legal, regulatory, accounting or reporting requirements.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure. However, no internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your Rights

Depending on applicable data protection law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including the right to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or other information about how your personal data is being processed.

Where UK data protection law applies, these rights are subject to the requirements and exemptions provided by applicable legislation.

10. External Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites and services. Syvaré Capital is not responsible for the privacy practices, security or content of third-party websites. We encourage visitors to review the privacy information provided by those services.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services, integrations or applicable requirements.

The latest version will be published on this page together with its most recent update date.
